Ladder fuels rather than canopy volumes consistently predict wildfire severity even in extreme topographic-weather conditions

Edit · Add comment

Christopher R. Hakkenberg, Matthew L. Clark, Tim Bailey, Patrick Burns, Scott J. Goetz · Communications Earth & Environment 2024 · 2024

What they did

Uses GEDI spaceborne LiDAR over 42 California wildfires (2019–2021) to show that ladder fuels, rather than canopy volume, consistently predict wildfire severity, even in extreme topographic and weather conditions.
