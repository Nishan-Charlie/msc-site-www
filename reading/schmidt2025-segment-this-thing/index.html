Segment This Thing: Foveated Tokenization for Efficient Point-Prompted Segmentation

Edit · Add comment

Tanner Schmidt, Richard Newcombe · CVPR 2025 · 2025

DOI arXiv

Status
To read
Topics
budgeted-inference, other
Feeds
P2 · NeurIPS 2027

What they did

Makes point-prompted image segmentation efficient by foveation: a crop around the prompt is tokenized with patches that become coarser with distance from the prompt, giving far fewer tokens than uniform patches.

My note

Allocates image resolution around a point prompt. Relevant to later targeted spatial queries.
